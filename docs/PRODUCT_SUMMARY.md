# Product Summary

## Elevator Pitch

A privacy-first iOS app that helps people make better decisions through reflection, not judgment. Grounded in behavioral economics, designed for humans.

## The Problem

People make impulsive decisions they later regret, not because they're irrational, but because cognitive biases shape how we think about choices in the moment. Traditional decision tools either:

- Moralize behavior (budgeting apps that shame spending)
- Oversimplify complexity (yes/no decision matrices)
- Require too much effort (detailed pros/cons lists)
- Feel clinical and detached

## Our Solution

A 90-second reflection process that:

1. Asks 5 carefully designed questions
2. Surfaces hidden costs and trade-offs
3. Provides personalized, non-judgmental insight
4. Respects user autonomy

**We don't tell you what to do. We help you see what you're actually choosing.**

## Target Audience

### Primary Users
- **Age:** 25-45
- **Psychographic:** Self-aware, interested in self-improvement
- **Behavior:** Occasionally makes impulsive purchases or commitments they regret
- **Values:** Privacy, autonomy, evidence-based approaches

### Use Cases

**Sarah, 32, Marketing Manager**
Considering a $400 standing desk. Sees it's on sale, feels urgency. Uses the app, realizes she's reacting to a stressful week, not actual need. Decides to wait. Saves money and mental clutter.

**James, 28, Software Engineer**
Thinking about subscribing to another streaming service. App helps him see he's trading money for content he'll rarely use. Chooses to rotate subscriptions instead.

**Maya, 41, Small Business Owner**
Evaluating whether to commit to a mastermind group ($3k/year). App surfaces that she's trading time and money. Helps her realize she values the accountability, not just the content. Proceeds with confidence.

## Core Features (v1)

### 1. Category Selection
Choose what kind of decision you're facing:
- Purchases
- Investments
- Time & Energy
- Experiences

### 2. Two Question Flows (A/B)
Each category has two scientifically designed 5-question flows:
- **Flow A:** Reflection-first (introspective)
- **Flow B:** Trade-off-first (economic)

Random assignment enables future optimization.

### 3. Personalized Reflection
After answering, receive a structured reflection:
- **Neutral Summary** — What you said, rephrased
- **Trade-off Spotlight** — What you're actually choosing
- **Future Reflection** — How this might feel later
- **Optional Soft Pause** — Non-directive suggestion to wait

### 4. Privacy-First Architecture
- All data stored locally
- No accounts, no tracking
- No cloud sync
- User controls deletion

## What Makes This Special

### Behavioral Economics Foundation

Every question targets specific cognitive biases:

- **Present Bias** — Overweighting immediate rewards
- **Loss Aversion** — Fear of losing what we have
- **Opportunity Cost Neglect** — Ignoring alternatives
- **Projection Bias** — Overestimating future preferences
- **Affect Heuristic** — Emotion-driven decisions

### Non-Judgmental Design

**We never:**
- Tell users what to do
- Label decisions as good/bad
- Score or rank decisions
- Use gamification or streaks

**We always:**
- Use neutral, intelligent language
- Respect autonomy
- Acknowledge complexity
- Preserve privacy

### Kahneman-Inspired Philosophy

**"The goal isn't perfect rationality — just fewer wrong decisions."**

This isn't about optimization or self-improvement. It's about seeing your choices more clearly.

## Risks & Mitigations

### Risk: Users find it preachy
**Mitigation:** Extensive tone testing, neutral language guidelines

### Risk: Too much friction
**Mitigation:** 5 questions max, 90 seconds, minimal typing

### Risk: Privacy concerns
**Mitigation:** Local-first, no accounts, transparent data practices

### Risk: Lack of engagement
**Mitigation:** Valuable in-the-moment, not habit-forming design
